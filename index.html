01 / THE DEVICE↘

More face to face.
Less phone to phone.

Babellino is a self-contained offline AI translator in development. The dedicated device combines an 8-inch touchscreen with on-device AI for live, face-to-face translation, without internet during use.

No connection to find. No phone to pass around. Just a little more room to understand each other.

About Babellino.

Currently in development. Pricing and availability have not been announced.

Does Babellino need a subscription or internet?

No subscription is required. Babellino is being developed for offline translation, with AI running on the device and no internet connection needed during use.

Which languages will Babellino support?

Supported languages have not been announced. The translated headlines and sample conversation on this page are illustrative, not a list of supported languages.

Is this the final hardware?

No. The illustration shows an 8-inch touchscreen concept. Final hardware specifications are still to come.
